Your Credit Limit will be activated within 1-2 business days after your application is approved. You will receive an SMS notification once it's ready to use.

You can use your Credit Limit through the CUB Vietnam App, online banking, or at any ATM. Simply select the Credit Limit option when making transactions.

The Credit Limit amount depends on your income, credit history, and other financial factors. Our team will assess your application and determine the appropriate limit for your profile.

Your Credit Limit is valid as long as your account remains active and in good standing. The limit will be reviewed annually and may be adjusted based on your usage and creditworthiness.

Your monthly payment is due on the same date each month as specified in your contract. You will receive a reminder notification 3 days before the due date.

You can make payments through auto-debit, online banking, mobile app, bank transfer, or visit any CUB Vietnam branch. We recommend setting up auto-debit for convenience.

Late payment fees will apply as per your contract terms. Your credit score may also be affected. Please contact us immediately if you anticipate payment difficulties.

Yes, you can make early payments without penalty. Early payments will reduce your outstanding balance and may save you on interest charges.

You can set up automatic payments through the CUB Vietnam App or by visiting any branch. You'll need to provide your preferred bank account details for auto-debit.

Payment notifications are sent via SMS, email, and push notifications through the CUB Vietnam App. You can customize your notification preferences in the app settings.

Payment reminders are sent 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before your due date. You'll also receive a confirmation once payment is processed successfully.

Yes, you can update your notification preferences anytime through the CUB Vietnam App under Settings > Notifications, or by contacting customer service.

Please check your spam folder and ensure your contact information is up to date. Contact our support team at 028 3622 6836 if you continue to miss notifications.

Payment notices are available in both Vietnamese and English. You can select your preferred language in the app settings or during account setup.

You can update your personal information through the CUB Vietnam App or by visiting any branch with required documents. Some changes may require verification.

You'll need a valid ID (CCCD/CMND), proof of income, and proof of residence. Additional documents may be required based on your application type.

To close your account, please visit any CUB Vietnam branch with your ID and account information. Ensure all outstanding balances are cleared before closure.
